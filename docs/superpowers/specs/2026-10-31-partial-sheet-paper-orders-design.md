# Paper orders on a partial sheet (Lambda, 2026-10-31)

Alpha ruling under the operator's "make it an extra and not a prerequisite". Paper only. Change id `partial-sheet-orders-v1`; kill knob `OFFICE_PARTIAL_SHEET_ORDERS` (default on; `0` returns exactly the known-only gate).

## What changes, what does not
- Changes: (1) `PaperService.enter` may build a PARTIAL receipt from the stored ticket when the full sheet failed for an ageing/proof reason; (2) the sells-60 s input of the risk check on those orders.
- Unchanged: role/epoch/incarnation, clip, concurrency, daily halt, kill, exits, authority-closed, sell-probe-ok, a PROVEN zero of sells, and the strict fill proof (a partial sheet never turns a failed strict quote into a fill). No new call, lock or wait on the fill path; the one tape read runs in the seat's order call. The full sheet, `MINT_MAX_AGE_S`, `desk_near_pass.py`, `tools/sheet.py` and `ps_asof` are untouched (their code hash is unchanged).
- Allowed failure classes: `mint_receipt_too_old_or_invalid`, `mint_receipt_unusable`, `tape_loss_since_cut`, `tape_commit_unproven`, `evidence_not_pass_at_read`, `pool_state_unproven`. Everything else (`ticket_incomplete`, `ticket_not_eligible`, clock, role) keeps the old refusal. A ticket must be PASS or in the near-pass stratum (same `near_pass_blockers` rule as the full sheet).

## The receipt
Same `receipts` format and verify path as a full sheet (`SH._verify`, `EO._receipt`, `freeze_bundle`, `risk_link`, `T.execute_decision` run unchanged): `sheet_kind: partial`, `cache_key` over a request that includes `sheet_kind`, `failure_class`, `change_id` and the build time; body = `safety` from the STORED evidence (no hard-filter re-run), `pool_state.base_decimals` from the stored mint receipt (shown with its age), the partial view (`partial_sheet`), the sells record, and the note "No current-liquidity evidence exists for this order beyond the strict fill proof."

## Sells (the risk wall input)
Recomputed read-only from the PumpSwap tape for the 60 s BEFORE THE TICKET'S CUT (not before order time; for an old ticket that proves the pool was sold into at the cut, not that it is sellable now):
- sells seen -> true, `sells_basis = cut_tape`, count stored;
- proven zero (rows seen, one `ps_pool_selection` row spanning the window, one capture interval with a non-NULL frontier past the cut, every loss in the window `complete`-scope with a readable non-empty pool list not naming the pool, no capture point) -> false -> risk vetoes `no_sells_60s`;
- unknown (no rows = outside retention or never stored, loss in the window, unreadable scope, capture point, capture not continuous, tape unreadable) -> null; allowed ONLY when the stored sell probe passed: `sells_basis = sell_probe` (stored probe age at order and its round-trip loss); otherwise risk vetoes `sells_60s_unknown`. Unknown is never stored as zero or as passed-by-tape.
`risk.py` change: one clause, `sells_unknown_allowed_by_probe` (set only by this builder inside the hashed receipt body) lets `sells_exist_60s = None` through; `False` still vetoes first.

## Stratification (queryable, not only labels)
Additive ledger migration (like `gate_basis`): `orders.sheet_state`, `sheet_class`, `sheet_ticket_age_s`, `sheet_unknown_n`, `sheet_view_hash`, `sells_basis` (`fresh_flow` | `cut_tape` | `sell_probe` partition the stamped orders), `sells_n`, `probe_age_s`, `probe_loss_pct`. NO column default: the migration backfills orders that exist when a column is first added to `known` / `fresh_flow` (they predate this change by definition), once; a later reopen does not touch a NULL. Every order submitted through `PaperService.enter` carries its stratum in the SAME transaction as its insert (`submit` writes it from the order metadata; an invalid stratum raises and creates no order), so an order cannot exist without it; known-sheet orders carry `known` / `fresh_flow` explicitly. An order created any other way stays NULL = unstamped, stratum unknown: counted and listed beside the strata in every report, never folded into `known`. The same facts are also in the order labels. The canary (first 10 partial orders, by `sheet_state = 'partial'` in submission order) is reported alone, with any NULL-stratum order beside it. `sell_probe` orders are the weakest-evidence stratum and are reported on their own; known and partial orders are never pooled in entry price, fills, rails or PnL. The first 10 partial orders are a canary reported alone. Per stratum report: attempts, refusals by reason, fills, delayed vs immediate, censored, ticket age at order, exit kinds.

## Measured yield (method)
Sample: the 600 most recent distinct desk tickets (`desk_tickets`, newest first) on 2026-10-31; 504 carry hard-filter evidence with a market cut. Of those, 345 are neither PASS nor in the near-pass stratum (never orderable, any sheet); 159 are PASS or near-pass. Applying only the guards this change touches (authority closed, sell probe ok, sells rule above, live tape read for the 60 s before each cut): 157 of the 159 pass (99 percent): 60 on `cut_tape`, 97 on `sell_probe` (sells unknown: 90 no rows in the window, 6 pool loss in the window, 1 capture point), 2 vetoed by a PROVEN zero. This is a prerequisite count, not an orderable count: role, caps, halt, FX and the strict fill proof are not run. It replaces the earlier 16 percent, which counted only tickets with sells seen on tape (80 of 505).

## Rollback
`OFFICE_PARTIAL_SHEET_ORDERS=0` (restart of the paper service) returns exactly the known-only gate. The ledger columns stay (additive, defaulted).
